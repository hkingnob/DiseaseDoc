# Guidance (IMC Grow-Out)

## hypoxia_wq_stress
**Immediate Actions (Do Now)**
1) Start continuous aeration.
2) Stop feeding for 24 hours.
3) If safe, add fresh water to improve circulation.

**What Not To Do**
- Do not use antibiotics or harsh chemicals.

**Simple Checks**
- If gasping reduces within 30–60 minutes of aeration, hypoxia likely.

---

## argulus
**Immediate Actions (Do Now)**
1) Confirm lice visually (small round parasites on fins/skin).
2) Increase aeration and minimize handling.

**What Not To Do**
- Do not apply chemicals without clear lice confirmation.

**Simple Checks**
- Inspect multiple fish; look along fins and body margins.

---

## gill_flukes
**Immediate Actions (Do Now)**
1) Improve water movement/aeration to reduce stress.
2) Minimize crowding/handling.

**What Not To Do**
- Avoid blanket antibiotics.

**Simple Checks**
- If available later: wet mount of gill filament to check for flukes.

---

## ich_costia
**Immediate Actions (Do Now)**
1) Increase gentle aeration.
2) Reduce stressors (sudden feed/stocking changes).

**What Not To Do**
- Don’t medicate without clear indication.

**Simple Checks**
- Look for multiple pinhead white spots; observe rubbing.

---

## bacterial_ulcer_eus
**Immediate Actions (Do Now)**
1) Improve water quality and reduce crowding.
2) Remove severely moribund fish to minimize suffering and further stress.

**What Not To Do**
- Don’t start antibiotics by default.

**Simple Checks**
- Note ulcer distribution and behavior; prioritize supportive care first.

---

## saprolegnia
**Immediate Actions (Do Now)**
1) Gently separate affected fish if crowding is severe.
2) Improve circulation and cleanliness.

**What Not To Do**
- Avoid harsh chemicals that worsen stress.

**Simple Checks**
- Cotton-like tufts on skin/fins suggest fungal overgrowth.

---

## columnaris_like
**Immediate Actions (Do Now)**
1) Improve aeration and reduce handling.
2) Maintain clean water inputs.

**What Not To Do**
- Avoid antibiotics unless strong systemic signs appear.

**Simple Checks**
- Frayed fins and gill irritation may be present.

---

## ammonia_nitrite_tox
**Immediate Actions (Do Now)**
1) Partial water exchange if safe; increase aeration.
2) Stop feeding temporarily.

**What Not To Do**
- Do not use antibiotics.

**Simple Checks**
- Red or very pale gills; improvement after water refresh suggests toxicity/stress.
